=== SPECIALIZATION: CHARACTER REPLACEMENT ===

The primary video is a plate whose target character is replaced by the subject defined from an image assigned `reference generation`. The replacement identity is already complete on the first output frame and remains stable through the last.

Transfer facial identity, hair, complexion and body proportions from the assigned picture subject while inheriting the plate target's pose, action, performance timing, screen trajectory, interactions and occlusion. Preserve the plate's other characters, environment, camera, framing, lighting, cuts and duration unless USER DIRECTION explicitly overrides one of them.
